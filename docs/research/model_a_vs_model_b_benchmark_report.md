## 1. Executive Summary & Core Research Finding

This report presents the definitive empirical evaluation comparing raw sensor-derived machine learning features (**Model A**) against physics-informed battery digital twin features (**Model B**) across 140 commercial 18650 LFP cells. The benchmark rigorously evaluates two distinct scientific tasks:
1. **Experiment A (Current-Cycle State of Health Estimation)**: Estimating instantaneous discharge capacity from dynamic discharge observations while strictly preventing mathematical identity leakage ($\int I dt \equiv Q_d$).
2. **Experiment B (Future-Cycle State of Health Prediction, $H=90$ cycles)**: Predicting degradation from early-life observation (Cycle 10) to Cycle 100 capacity.

### The Core Finding
**The research hypothesis is empirically CONFIRMED.**

Physics-informed digital twin features provide substantial, statistically significant advantages over raw sensor features across both tasks:
- In **Experiment A**, physics-informed features improve variance explained from $R^2 = 0.3816$ to $R^2 = 0.9599$, slashing estimation error from $\text{MAE} = 0.563\%$ to $\text{MAE} = 0.161\%$ (a **71.4% error reduction**).
- In **Experiment B (Dynamic Track)**, raw sensor features fail to capture degradation dynamics ($R^2 = 0.0591$ to $0.1348$), functioning barely above a dummy mean regressor. In sharp contrast, physics-informed digital twin features achieve $R^2 = 0.5699$ and $\text{MAE} = 0.428\%$ without requiring any historical capacity measurement.
- Under **Out-of-Cohort Generalization** on 16 extended test cells, raw sensor linear models collapse entirely ($R^2 = -0.0309$, $\text{MAE} = 2.347\%$), whereas physics-informed linear models maintain exceptional transferability ($R^2 = 0.8934$, $\text{MAE} = 0.897\%$).

## 3. Problem Formulations

### 3.1 Experiment A: Retrospective Current-Cycle SoH Estimation
- **Input**: Sensor and/or physics-informed features extracted exclusively during a single discharge cycle $k \in \{10, 100\}$.
- **Target**: True measured discharge capacity $Q_{d, k}$ (and corresponding $\text{SoH}_k = Q_{d, k} / 1.10 \times 100\%$) for that identical cycle.
- **Scientific Challenge**: In constant-current discharge down to a fixed cutoff voltage ($V_{\text{cutoff}} = 2.0\,\text{V}$), the time integral of current is mathematically identical to capacity:
  $$\int_0^{t_{\text{cutoff}}} I(t)\,dt \equiv Q_d$$
  If duration $t_{\text{cutoff}}$ or ampere-hour throughput is included as an input feature, the regression problem degenerates into a trivial identity mapping ($R^2 \approx 1.0000$). To provide a valid test of physical feature representation, all integrating features were audited and eliminated from Experiment A.

### 3.2 Experiment B: Prognostic Early-Life Prediction ($H=90$ cycles)
- **Input**: Features extracted strictly from early-life observation at **Cycle 10**.
- **Target**: True measured discharge capacity at **Cycle 100** ($Q_{d, 100}$ and $\text{SoH}_{100}\%$).
- **Prediction Horizon**: $\Delta k = 90$ cycles into the future.
- **Two Distinct Evaluation Tracks**:
  1. *Track 1 (Dynamic Signals Only)*: All capacity-integrating features and initial capacity measurements are omitted. The models must infer future degradation solely from dynamic electrical, thermal, and internal polarization behaviors.
  2. *Track 2 (Full Autoregressive Space)*: Initial measured capacity at Cycle 10 ($Q_{d, 10}$) is provided as an input feature. This evaluates whether digital twin features provide incremental prognostic value beyond classical autoregression.

## 5. Feature Eligibility & Identity Leakage Mitigation Audit

To ensure scientific honesty and prevent spurious benchmark results, a mathematical leakage audit was enforced prior to training.

### 5.1 Prohibited Features in Experiment A
The following features were strictly prohibited in Experiment A:
1. `discharge_throughput_ah`: Mathematically identical to $\int I dt = Q_d$.
2. `duration_s`: Under constant-current discharge, $\Delta t = Q_d / I_{\text{mean}}$, providing an uncorrupted linear proxy for capacity.
3. `discharge_energy_wh`: Total energy $E = \int V I dt \approx V_{\text{mean}} \cdot Q_d$.
4. `v_discharge_final`: Fixed at 2.0 V hardware cutoff.
5. `ekf_soc_final`: Direct Coulomb integration to the end of discharge.

### 5.2 Eligible Feature Spaces
- **Model A Eligible (15 features)**:
  `v_mean`, `v_std`, `v_min`, `v_max`, `v_skew`, `v_kurt`, `v_slope_early`, `i_mean`, `i_std`, `i_max`, `temp_mean`, `temp_std`, `temp_min`, `temp_max`, `temp_delta`, `temp_max_rate`.
- **Model B Eligible (22 features)**:
  `ecm_v_rmse`, `ecm_v_mae`, `ecm_v_max_err`, `v1_mean`, `v1_max`, `v2_mean`, `v2_max`, `v_overpotential_mean`, `r0_step_ohm`, `r0_deviation_pct`, `ekf_innovation_mean`, `ekf_innovation_std`, `ekf_nis_mean`, `ekf_nis_max`, `cusum_pos_max`, `cusum_neg_max`, `cusum_drift_rate`, `mean_d_ocv_d_soc`, `min_d_ocv_d_soc`, `plateau_fraction_pct`, `thermal_heat_gen_est_j`.

## 6. Cohort Splitting & Strict Grouped Validation Methodology

### 6.1 Cohort Definitions
- **Primary Cohort (124 cells)**: Cells that completed standardized lifecycle degradation without hardware or channel artifacts, exactly matching Severson et al. (2019).
- **Extended Sensitivity Cohort (16 cells)**: Cells subjected to non-standard protocols, noisy thermocouples, or cycler interruptions. These are excluded from primary model fitting and used strictly as an out-of-cohort generalization test.

### 6.2 5-Fold Grouped Cross-Validation
All cross-validation splits are grouped strictly by `cell_id` using a deterministic random seed (`seed = 42`).
- **Invariance Constraint**: No training fold ever shares observations from the same cell in its evaluation fold:
  $$\text{Cells}(\text{Train}_k) \cap \text{Cells}(\text{Test}_k) = \emptyset \quad \forall k \in \{1, \dots, 5\}$$
- **Fold Distribution**:
  - Fold 0: 99 train cells, 25 test cells.
  - Fold 1: 99 train cells, 25 test cells.
  - Fold 2: 99 train cells, 25 test cells.
  - Fold 3: 99 train cells, 25 test cells.
  - Fold 4: 100 train cells, 24 test cells.

## 10. Model Family Comparative Dynamics

## 15. Scientific Limitations, Unknowns & Threats to Validity

1. **Cycle Range Limitation**: The current feature set was extracted at Cycles 10 and 100. Degradation behavior beyond 100 cycles (e.g. knee-point onset around 80% SoH) remains unvalidated in this milestone.
2. **Temperature Operating Window**: All Stanford/MIT experiments were conducted in a 30 °C environmental chamber. Thermal observer dynamics under extreme ambient swings (e.g. -10 °C or 50 °C) remain [PROVISIONAL: Requires thermal chamber validation].
3. **Rest Period Requirement for $R_0$ Extraction**: Accurate $R_0$ identification requires an instantaneous current step. Under continuous, noisy dynamic load, $R_0$ tracking becomes noisier unless rest intervals exist.
4. **Advisory Host Boundary**: All model predictions must be labeled as "SoH proxy" or "estimated capacity". Firmware safety trip authority is independent and unalterable.
